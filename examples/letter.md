Dear Dr. Rivera,

Thank you for taking the time to discuss **md-pdf** at last week's open-source
meetup. As promised, I am writing to propose a formal collaboration between
Rivera Labs and our team around the project.

## Why collaborate

md-pdf converts Markdown to professional PDFs using [Typst](https://typst.app),
with a flexible template system and OKF-compatible front matter. We believe a
joint effort could bring three concrete benefits:

- **Shared templates** — a common library of branded, reusable templates.
- **Faster releases** — pooled maintenance and review capacity.
- **Wider reach** — combined documentation and community outreach.

## Proposed next steps

1. A short kickoff call to align on goals and scope.
2. A two-week pilot integrating md-pdf into one of your documentation pipelines.
3. A joint review of the results before committing to a longer roadmap.

I have attached a draft roadmap and a rough budget estimate for your review.
I would be glad to adjust either to fit your priorities.

Please let me know which dates work for a kickoff call in the coming weeks. I
look forward to working together.
